# My First Post

Welcome to my blog! This is the very first post on this site, and I'm excited to finally have a place to share my thoughts, projects, and creative work.

I've been meaning to set up a blog for a while now. There's something nice about having a corner of the internet that's entirely your own — no algorithm deciding what gets seen, no character limits, just words on a page.

## What to Expect

I'll be writing about a mix of things: coding projects I'm working on, art and illustration, and whatever else catches my interest. Posts might be long and detailed or short and casual depending on the topic.

If you're reading this, thanks for stopping by. More posts are on the way.
