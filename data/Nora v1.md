### **Nora - Comprehensive Character Profile**

- **Full Name:** Nora [Harloni Surname based on Father's Origin + Rank/Job, e.g., "Nora Hapren"] (He likely uses a simplified, Krosh-style alias in some situations)
- **Age:** 17
- **Height:** 5'9" (175 cm)
- **Build:** Lean. Not scrawny, but lacks the bulk of someone who does physical labor. Has the wiry strength of someone who spends hours tinkering with projects. After his ordeal, he becomes gaunt.
- **Appearance:**
    - **Face:** Sharp, intelligent eyes that observe everything. They were once bright with curiosity but are now shadowed with cynicism and grief. Dark circles are a permanent feature after Harlon. Hair is dark and often unruly, as he runs his hands through it when thinking.
    - **Clothing:** In Krosh, he wore comfortable, modern-ish but modest clothes—well-fitting jeans, solid-colored t-shirts. In Harlon, his clothes become more worn, chosen for practicality and to not draw attention. In Arette, he initially wears donated, simple, brightly colored garments that feel alien to him.
- **Personality:**
    - **Core Self:** A good person hardened by circumstances. Fundamentally decent and honorable, but his desperation forces him to make difficult choices. He is a realist, almost a pessimist, after his experiences, but a sliver of hope remains, which is kindled in Arette.
    - **Before Exile:** Inquisitive, tech-obsessed, slightly naive about how the world works. Loyal to his friends and family, with a dry, geeky sense of humor.
    - **After Exile:** Cautious, observant, internalizes his stress. He is quick to distrust kindness, as seen with his initial reaction to Arette. He is resilient and adaptable, a survivor above all else.
- **Traits:**
    - **Positive:** Intelligent, resourceful, resilient, loyal, observant.
    - **Negative:** Cynical, distrustful, prone to internalizing pain, desperate.
- **Habits:**
    - Fidgets with small objects (screws, bits of wire) when thinking or anxious.
    - Constantly observes and analyzes his surroundings, a habit born from the danger in Harlon.
    - In Arette, he starts **keeping a journal** meticulously. This is both a coping mechanism and a way to satisfy his burning curiosity.
    - Has a tendency to shut down emotionally when overwhelmed, retreating into his own mind or his projects.
- **Likes:**
    - Robotics and engineering (his passion and escape).
    - Problem-solving and learning how things work.
    - The comfort and stability he once had in Krosh.
    - His friends' nerdy, enthusiastic conversations.
    - The genuine, unasked-for kindness he experiences in Arette (though he initially distrusts it).
- **Dislikes:**
    - Injustice and corruption (a deep-seated hatred formed in Harlon).
    - Feeling powerless and helpless.
    - People who are wilfully ignorant or who buy into propaganda (like the "pretty lie" of Krosh).
    - Being pitied.
    - The feeling of being an outsider, which he experiences in both Harlon and initially in Arette.

### **Friends & Family (Short Profiles)**

#### **Family**

- **Mother:** A Harloni woman by birth, fierce and protective. She embodies the theme of "financial desperation but still having dignity." She is pragmatic (selling her gold for forged papers) and has a fiery temper (beating up the neighbor). Her advice, _"Study for a year then get out,"_ is Nora's driving motivation.
- **Father:** A Kroshi citizen of Harloni descent. A quiet, dignified man worn down by the struggle to protect his family. He is loving but practical (giving Nora the basic button phone for safety). He represents the silent struggle of parents trying to provide in an impossible situation. He acts as the lookout during the mother's confrontation, showing his supportive, if less confrontational, role.

#### **Friends (in Krosh)**

- **June:** The tech genius of the group. Serious and focused. He is the one who takes the initiative to call Nora's mom and piece together the situation logically. He is deeply concerned and loyal.
- **Sen:** The hacker/information gatherer. Inquisitive and sharp, he's the one digging into satellite data and public signals. He is skeptical and has a dry wit ("the hell you mean?").
- **Leo:** The "heart" of the group. Less technical but more intuitive and socially aware. He provides the crucial, out-of-the-box thinking ("Like a Cell Tower") that the others might miss. He is the one who directly voices the fear everyone is avoiding: "You think he's dead?"
