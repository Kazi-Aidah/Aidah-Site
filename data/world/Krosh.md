## Kroshian Hair & Eye Colors
### **Hair Palette: "Maintained & Stylized"**
- **Blonde/Platinum**: Most common - shows prosperity, access to products
- **Light Brown/Caramel**: Second most common  
- **Dark Brown**: Less common but still well-maintained
- **Auburn/Copper**: Rarer, considered fashionable
- **Overall look**: Healthy shine, well-cut, styled

### **Eye Colors: "Bright & Clear"**
- **Light Blue**: Most common - clean, clear
- **Green**: Second most common - vibrant
- **Hazel**: Mixed, shows genetic diversity
- **Light Brown**: Warm but clear
- **Overall look**: Bright, alert, well-nourished

## Harloni Hair & Eye Colors
### **Hair Palette: "Practical & Weathered"**
- **Dark Brown**: Most common - practical, low-maintenance
- **Black**: Very common - genetic dominance
- **Auburn**: Less common but appears dull
- **Dirty Blonde**: Occasionally, but looks muted
- **Overall look**: Often unstyled, showing sun damage, premature graying

### **Eye Colors: "Deep & Shadowed"**
- **Dark Brown**: Most dominant - practical
- **Black**: Very common - deep-set
- **Dark Green**: Muted, not bright
- **Dark Hazel**: Shadowed, not clear
- **Overall look**: Often bloodshot, tired, showing stress

## **Nora's Unique Visual Identity**

### **Hair: The Middle Ground**
- **Color**: Dark brown (Kroshian) but with **natural wave** that's becoming more unruly
- **In Krosh**: Well-groomed, friends help style it
- **In Harlon**: Becomes increasingly messy, practical cuts
- **In Arette**: Returns to longer, but fundamentally changed

### **Eyes: The Story Teller**
- **Color**: **Dark hazel** - can pass in both groups
- **Shape**: More angular than his friends' rounder eyes
- **Expression**: 
  - **In Krosh**: Bright, curious, optimistic
  - **In Harlon**: Shadowed, suspicious, hardened
  - **In Arette**: Calculating, but hint of wonder returning

## **The Progression Storytelling**

### **Episodes 1-4: Color Harmony**
- Everyone in the friend group has **healthy, vibrant colors**
- Nora fits right in with his dark hazel eyes and wavy dark brown hair
- Colors scream "everything's fine" - bright blues, clear greens, healthy browns

### **Episodes 5-10: Color Dissonance**
- **Nora's hair**: Gradually loses shine, becomes more practical
- **Nora's eyes**: Start to show stress, lose their brightness
- **Friends**: Remain unchanged - still bright, clear, hopeful
- **Harloni people**: Introduced with muted, practical color palette

### **Episode 11: Devastating Contrast**
- **Friends**: Still exactly as bright and hopeful as Episode 1
- **Nora**: Muted colors reflecting his journey
- **The realization**: Color tells the story of time and trauma

## **Practical Animation Tips**

### **Color Symbolism**
- **Krosh**: Colors represent prosperity, stability, possibility
- **Harlon**: Colors represent harsh reality, survival, exhaustion  
- **Arette**: Colors represent natural, honest simplicity

### **Lighting Effects**
- **Krosh scenes**: Soft, natural lighting that makes colors pop
- **Harlon scenes**: Harsh, artificial lighting that washes out colors
- **Arette scenes**: Warm, golden lighting that makes muted colors beautiful

### **Character Introduction Strategy**
- **Episodes 1-4**: Let audiences fall in love with the bright, healthy colors
- **Episode 5**: Introduce Kroshians with "maintained" colors
- **Episode 6**: Introduce Harlonis with "practical" colors  
- **Episodes 7-10**: Show color transition in Nora as the "bridge" character

This system gives you instant character recognition while visually telling the story of loss, adaptation, and hope. Nora's unique color palette makes him stand out without making him feel like an outsider initially.
