**Race:** Human (Aether-touched)
**Age:** 27
**Appearance:** Silver-haired with stormy grey eyes, wears flowing blue robes adorned with cloud patterns

***

**Abilities:** 
- Aeromancy (air magic)
- Weather manipulation
- Cloud walking
- Storm calling
- <span style="text-align:center; background-color: #563;"> abysmal </span>

![[GuyHead.png]]

**Personality:** Calm and observant, but with a tempestuous inner spirit. Prefers solitude but fiercely protective of those she cares about.

**Background:** Born during a rare celestial convergence, Ciel has always had an affinity for the skies. She travels the world studying ancient weather patterns and forgotten cloud temples.
