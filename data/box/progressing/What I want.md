Krosh is a pretty lie. Harlon is an ugly truth. Arette represents hope.

Idea:
Nora, a 17 year old old living is Krosh with potential for robotics had to get out with his Harloni family despite never having ever set foot in Harlon AND DESPITE BEING A KROSHI CITIZEN. He is forced to leave his whole life, friends and promises behind or otherwise they'll be exiled forcefully.

Once they arrive in Harlon, they get no help even from family. They're forced to use their savings to rent an apartment and live there while the father looks for work. Nora understands the situation, sees the death and violence with his own eyes. His mother's words, "Study for a year then get out" rings in his head. He just needed to turn 18 and complete his 12th.

His parents enroll him to a high school there. Nora watches as stacks of money is given to the Principal. He sees how his father can't seem to find a job so he takes an action and puts his robotics projects online for money, desperation. The father still gives him a button phone, "for communication here, since smartphones can end up getting stolen."

Just as Nora is adjusting to this new environment, the Harloni government decides to exile all Kroshians as a response to Krosh's Harloni Expel. The parents try to hide him. "There's too many people in Harlon, they won't find you." They don't have much money, only the savings (that were for Nora's school), stakes have changed. The mother decides to sell her gold and valuables to get Nora's papers forged.

Only for a neighbour to rat them out.

Nora is taken to the prison to keep all Kroshians, then moved to a ship where he meets Roan and helps him.  Roan reveals, "They'll just send us as slaves to Yefie and call it an accident: Ship drowned in the sea".  Due to Nora helping Roan, Roan lets him on a secret; that he had tipped the guards and a speedboat's waiting for him.

On the other hand, the mother beats up the neighbour while the father stands guard.

They manage to get on it, days pass by, 3 weeks pass by in the sea, they're following this land they saw. (3 weeks on a speedboat? Is that realistic?) until a storm hits them and-

Nora ends up in the Aretten shores alone. He thinks he died. That he's just in heaven being treated so well, the villagers feeding him, treating him well, teaching him their language. Time passes by. It's too happy there, too good. It has to not be real. Only for Nora to see blood again.. 

Reminded by the scene he saw back in Harlon, and how everything *feels* real, he now gives the benefit of the doubt that it is, in fact, real. "But there was no 'Arette' on the world map, much less a whole empire?"

He starts keeping a journal, noting the phones the Elites use, too similar to Phones, the mechanics and everything. It helps him clear his mind and he begins to look for knowledge, more and more, to understand Arette. He knows he'll never see his family again but that doesn't mean he has to stay in a state of grieving.
