# KROSH <sub style="font-size: 22px;"><i>The pretty lie</i></sub>
Type: Country
Country Name: Krosh
Country Code: KR
Currency: Kaash (K)
**Economy:**
State capitalism; citizens enjoy a comfortable life while elites quietly profit
**Social State:** 
United, warm climate, good food, low visible crime (much is covered up), foreigners are often unwelcome.
**Government:** 
Appears benevolent, but tightly monitors and controls behind the scenes.
**Notable Characteristic:** Citizens believe life is good; the illusion hides corruption, exploitation, and power concentration.
**Quote:** “They like to give people the illusion of choice while keeping an eye on everything.”
**Language:** Low volume.
**Surname Examples:**
Armen, Kimish, Ralif, Thish
**Surname Style:** Easy, based on job. (Format: Consonant + -if, -ish, -en, -hef)
Architecture: Tall buildings, short houses, clean streets and good architecture overall. However, non-capital areas may be overlooked...

# HARLON <sub style="font-size: 22px;"><i>The ugly truth</i></sub>
Type: Republic
Country Name: Harlon
Country Code: HN
Currency: Lons
**Economy:**
Market economy driven by cartels and citizen labor, plus subsistence sectors.
**Social State:**
Unsafe, corrupt, heavily monitored; citizens are forced to comply with rules.
**Government:** 
Propaganda, secret police, censorship, widespread corruption.
**Notable Characteristic:** 
Life is harsh and controlled; the people have little choice and face systemic oppression.
**Quote:** 
“They do not give a choice. Just take and take, and the people are forced to accept that.”
**Language:** Soft sounding language.
**Surname Style:** Long, based on location+rank/job. (Format: the first letters of a location + abbreviated rank/job.)
**Surname Examples:** n/a
Architecture: Cramped tall buildings. Monotonous, some buildings have worn down colours. Lack of greenery.

# ARETTE  <sub style="font-size: 22px;"><i>The hope</i></sub>
Type: Constitutional Monarchy
Country Name: KA (Kingdom of Arette)
Government: Centralized monarchy with a council-based system

Main Head presides alongside State Heads and a Council of experts (doctor, engineer, judges, etc.)

Leaders are salaried, strictly accountable, and forbidden from exploiting citizens
**Economy:** 
Mixed/rebuilding economy; community-focused and meritocratic
**Social State:** 
Recovering from historical erasure; rebuilt by people who have suffered injustice
**Notable Characteristic:** 
This empire had been removed from the world map eons ago. Only leaders know of it, they have chosen to keep it a secret.
**Languages:** One official language + tons of minorities.
**Surname Style:** Based on region; some regions prefer location-based, date-meaning based, some similar herbs and natural resources.
**Architecture:** Every building has a fun and distinct colour. Low buildings, max 2 floors is, 3 floors is for a big family or halls or important national buildngs.
