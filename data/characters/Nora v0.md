# Nora Ertificer
**Full Name:** Nora Ertificer
- **Name Meaning:** "Nora" means Honor/Light. "Ert-" for his parents' home, Erton State (Harlon). "-ficer" refers to his father's skilled job (Electronics Technician).
- **Symbolism:** His name embodies his core conflict: a boy destined for "honor and light" who is defined by his heritage ("Ert") and the skills ("-ficer") that become his means of survival.

**Age:** 17

**Height:** Average for his age, but often seems smaller due to a habit of slouching or making himself seem inconspicuous.

**Build:** Lean. He had a comfortable, softness to him in Krosh, but hardship in Harlon has whittled him down to a resilient, wiry leanness. He's not muscular, but possesses a stubborn endurance.

**Appearance:**
- **Face:** Has expressive, intelligent eyes that are his most notable feature. In Krosh, they were bright and quick; in Harlon, they became hollow, haunted, and often distant. After arriving in Arette, a cautious, observant light returns to them.
- **Hair:** Dark, often messy—a practical neglect born from being lost in thought or simply too tired to care.
- **Clothing:** Prefers modest, practical clothing. In Krosh, it was clean and casual. In Harlon, his clothes became worn and dusty. In Arette, he wears the simple, functional garments given to him, always a bit too large, emphasizing his displacement.
- **Posture:** Carries the weight of his experiences in his shoulders. He often hunches slightly, as if trying to shield himself from the world.

### Personality
**Core Identity:** A compassionate and intellectually curious boy violently stripped of his innocence. He is grappling with profound trauma but is fundamentally driven by a deep-seated need to protect others and understand the world around him. His journey is one of shattering, survival, and the slow, painful reassembly of his spirit.

### Traits
- **Resourceful:** His first instinct in a crisis is to solve problems practically (fixing Roan's injury, setting up a Gumroad shop, taking apart the N-r-N phone to understand it).
- **Empathic:** Deeply feels the pain of others, which is both a source of his trauma and his motivation to help. He is haunted by the suffering he witnessed.
- **Observant:** Notice small details—the stack of money given to the principal, the specific model of a phone, the patterns in people's behavior. This makes him a quick learner but also hyper-aware of dangers.
- **Resilient:** Has an incredible capacity to endure emotional and physical pain, even when he is completely broken down.
- **Internalizing:** Tends to bottle up his emotions, leading to periods of numbness and dissociation. He struggles to ask for help for himself.
- **Inquisitive:** A natural learner and tinkerer. His mind seeks patterns, logic, and understanding as a way to cope with chaos.

### Habits
- **Pre-Trauma (Krosh):**
    - Humming while he works on projects.
    - Procrastinating by going on "side quests" (building robots instead of studying).
    - Easy, genuine laughter with friends and family.
- **During/Post-Trauma (Harlon/Arette):**
	- Biting his lip or the inside of his cheek when anxious or deep in thought.
	- Staring into the middle distance, becoming lost in painful memories.
	- Fidgeting with his hands or a small object when stressed.
	- Keeping a mental or physical journal, constantly analyzing his surroundings for clues and consistency ("Is this real?").
	- Offering help to others almost reflexively, as a way to regain a sense of agency.
### Likes
- **Robotics and Engineering:** The pure, logical joy of creation. It represents his lost potential and happier past.
- **His Family:** The memory of their warmth is his anchor. His little sister, Noorin, is a symbol of innocence he desperately wants to protect.
- **Learning:** The acquisition of knowledge is a safe harbor and a tool for survival.
- **Tangerines/Oranges:** A small, sensory memory of comfort and home.
- **Structure and Logic:** Spreadsheets, plans, and well-functioning systems—a stark contrast to the chaotic injustice he experienced.

### Dislikes
- **Injustice and Corruption:** A deep, visceral hatred for systems that abuse power and harm the innocent.
- **Helplessness:** The feeling of being powerless to change his or his family's situation is his greatest fear.
- **Betrayal:** The neighbor who reported him solidified his distrust of those who sacrifice others for personal gain.
- **Being a Burden:** His desperation to contribute financially in Harlon stems from a fear of worsening his family's plight.

### Character Arc Summary
Nora's arc is a journey from **Passive Victim to Active Agent**.

1. **Krosh:** A boy with **Agency** over his life (friends, hobbies, future).
2. **Harlon:** That agency is systematically stripped away, reducing him to a **Victim** of forces beyond his control.
3. **The Ship & Arette:** Through a final act of kindness (helping Roan), he **earns back a sliver of agency**. In Arette, he actively uses his core traits—curiosity, observation, and a desire to understand—to grapple with his new reality and slowly rebuild a purpose for himself: to heal, not to harm.
