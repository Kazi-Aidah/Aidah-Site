# The Kingdom of Krosh

## Geography
Nestled in the Dragon's Tooth Mountains, Krosh is a rugged nation built into the cliffs and valleys of one of the world's most formidable mountain ranges.

## Capital: Stonehaven
A city carved directly into the mountainside, known for its impressive stone architecture and underground networks.

## Economy
- **Mining:** Rich in gemstones and rare minerals
- **Stonecraft:** World-renowned stonemasons and architects
- **Defense:** Natural mountain barriers make it nearly impregnable

## Culture
Kroshians value endurance, craftsmanship, and tradition. Their society is organized into powerful clan families, each controlling different mountain regions.

## Notable Locations
- **The Crystal Depths:** Vast underground mines
- **Skybridge Pass:** Dangerous mountain trade route
- **The Stone Council:** Governing body of elder clan leaders
