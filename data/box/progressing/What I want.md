**Project: The "Stair-Smoothie" (Adaptive Mobility Aid)**
- **The Problem:** An elderly neighbor or a student at their school with a temporary injury struggles with a long flight of stairs.
- **The Build:** They don't build a full elevator. Instead, they create a **motorized, foldable seat** that attaches securely to the existing railing. The user sits, taps a button, and the device smoothly carries them up or down the track.
- **The "Cool/Techy" Part:**
    - **Nora (Builder):** Designs the mechanical clutch system that grips the railing safely. Uses 3D-printed custom gears.
    - **Sen (Hacker):** Writes the code for a failsafe system—ultrasonic sensors to detect obstacles and auto-stop.
    - **June (Architect):** Calculates the weight limits, stress points, and designs the foldable chassis for portability.
    - **Leo (Heart):** He's the one who noticed the problem and convinced them to help. He also designs the comfortable, ergonomic seat.
- **Visual Payoff:** A heartfelt scene where the person uses it successfully, with a wide smile. Shows technology solving a real human problem elegantly.

**Project: "Lumina-Swarm" (Interactive Light Installation)**
- **The Problem:** They want to create something stunning for the school's upcoming festival or art show.
- **The Build:** A **swarm of dozens of small, wireless LED "pixels"** that can be programmed to create complex, flowing patterns and animations in mid-air.
- **The "Cool/Techy" Part:**
    - **Nora (Builder):** Designs and solders each individual light unit, creating a modular system.
    - **Sen (Hacker):** Writes the complex swarm algorithm so the lights move in sync without a central controller, creating mesmerizing patterns.
    - **June (Architect):** Plans the large-scale physical structure to hang them and manages the wireless network so all units communicate.
    - **Leo (Heart):** Choreographs the light show to music, telling a simple emotional story with light and color.
- **Visual Payoff:** The climax is the art show itself. The lights dance beautifully, amazing the audience and showing the pure, joyful potential of their skills. This is a perfect project for the "Robotics Fair" you mentioned.
